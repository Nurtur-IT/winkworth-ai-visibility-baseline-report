05 — Key insights

01
Winkworth holds only 3% of total AI citations despite operating 100+ offices nationally. KFH — smaller, more localised agents — outperform Winkworth on AI visibility, making this a structural content and authority issue, not a brand size issue.
02
Gemini zero-citation is the single most urgent priority. Google's own AI returning zero results for Winkworth points to fundamental E-E-A-T gaps that simultaneously suppress Google AI Overview performance and organic rankings. Savills leads Gemini with 235 citations.
03
Savills' dominance (2,347 citations, 37% share) sets the bar for what GEO-mature content achieves. Their strength in Google AIO (684) and Grok (1.1K) points to a combination of deep structured data, authoritative press coverage, and a strong social/X presence — all directly replicable for Winkworth.
04
Grok is Winkworth's relative bright spot and fastest available quick win. At 75 citations, Winkworth ranks 5th of 7 on Grok — the only platform where it escapes last place. Amplifying content through X/Twitter and social channels can compound this advantage rapidly while other optimisations take effect.

06 — Recommended actions

P1 — Immediate
Deploy structured data across all key pages
Implement Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, and RealEstateListing schema on winkworth.co.uk. This directly feeds Google AI Overview and Gemini and is the highest-ROI technical GEO action available immediately.
P1 — Immediate
Establish and expand Wikipedia & Wikidata presence
LLMs heavily weight Wikipedia as a training source. A comprehensive, factual Wikipedia entry will lift ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity citations substantially. Savills' and Knight Frank's strong LLM presence correlates directly with robust Wikipedia entries.
P1 — Immediate
Resolve Gemini zero-citation root cause
Audit E-E-A-T signals in full: author credentials on key pages, Google Business Profile completeness, and press coverage volume. Zero Gemini citations indicate trust signal deficiencies requiring immediate technical and content diagnosis.
P2 — 30 days
Publish AI-optimised FAQ & editorial content
Create long-form, question-led content targeting the prompt battery categories. Structure answers in 40–60 word paragraphs that AI systems can confidently extract and cite in responses. Target Perplexity first for fastest impact.
P2 — 30 days
Configure GA4 AI referrer tracking
Set up a custom channel group for AI traffic sources in GA4 (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Bing AI, Gemini referrals). Establish the measurement baseline now so that citation improvements can be correlated with traffic and lead volume gains.
P3 — 60 days
Build press & brand mention authority
Increase coverage in Estates Gazette, Property Week, Evening Standard, and BBC News. Third-party brand mentions are a primary LLM training signal. Savills' category leadership correlates strongly with volume of authoritative press citations.
